# 📊 Sales Analysis Dashboard (Excel Project)

## Overview
This project presents a **Sales Dashboard** built in Microsoft Excel to analyze company performance across multiple regions, products, and customer segments.  
It provides interactive visualizations and key metrics to support **data-driven decision making**.

---

## Features
- **Key Metrics**: Revenue, Profit, Cost, Average Sales, Top Product
- **Trend Analysis**: Profit over Time (monthly line chart)
- **Product Performance**: Bar chart comparison across products
- **Category Insights**: Furniture, Office Supplies, Tech
- **Sales Channels**: Online vs Retail (donut chart)
- **Customer Segments**: Consumer, Corporate, Small Business
- **Regional Breakdown**: East, North, South, West

---

## Dataset
- Source: Company sales records (sample data)
- Format: Excel tables with structured fields
- Columns include:
  - Date
  - Region
  - Product
  - Category
  - Sales Channel
  - Customer Segment
  - Revenue, Cost, Profit

---

## Dashboard Highlights
- 📈 **Profit Trend**: Clear upward growth toward year-end  
- 🏆 **Top Product**: Product D with highest contribution  
- 🛒 **Category Split**: Furniture and Tech dominate sales  
- 🌍 **Regional View**: Balanced distribution across East, North, South, West
